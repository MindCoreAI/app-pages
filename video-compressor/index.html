Privacy Policy

Video Compressor is designed to process videos locally. The developer does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, or video-processing server for this app.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or video content through the app.

Photos And Files

  • Photo Library access is used only to display videos you can choose and to save a compressed video when you request it.
  • You may instead use Apple’s private picker or Files interface to choose a video.
  • The selected video and compressed result remain on your device. Temporary working files are stored in the app’s local container.
  • The original video is not modified by the app.

App Preferences

Language preference, compression settings, and the one-time in-app promotion state are stored locally on your device.

External Links

The Settings screen may link to App Store pages for other apps by the same developer. Opening those links is optional and is handled by Apple.

Deleting Data

Temporary files may be removed by the system or when a new compression begins. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences and files. Videos you saved to Photos or Files remain there until you delete them.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

Terms Of Use

Video Compressor is provided as a general-purpose media utility. Compression can reduce visual quality, and estimated output sizes may differ slightly from final results because video formats include variable overhead.

You are responsible for having the right to process and share the videos you select. Keep a copy of important originals and verify compressed results before deleting any source media.
