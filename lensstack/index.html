Getting started

  1. Choose the free 14-day trial and confirm the US$0.00 non-consumable trial purchase with the App Store, or choose the one-time lifetime unlock.
  2. Allow Camera access when requested. Frame loosely and hold the phone steady while capturing.
  3. In Reframe, pinch and pan the Combined view, inspect individual Sources, or Compare originals with independent zoom and position.
  4. Choose a crop and use Save Photo to export the visible framing to Photos. Compare's Save Sheet exports a labeled comparison image, not the Combined photo.

Saved captures

LensStack keeps one editable stack and its lens originals in app-local storage. A successful new capture replaces the previous stack. Export any frames you want to keep before capturing again. Photos already exported are separate and are not removed when the app replaces its editable stack.

When trial access ends, locally stored originals are preserved, but viewing, editing, and exporting them in LensStack require the lifetime unlock. Removing the app deletes its local stack and preferences, subject to any operating-system backup you later restore. Exported images remain in Photos until you delete them there.

Camera and image quality

Available lenses vary by iPhone. Three simultaneous live views require compatible hardware and may be unavailable when temperature or system resource limits restrict camera use. Use Single preview when offered; the available still-camera sources can still be captured.

For best results, use good light, a still scene, and subjects away from the phone. Nearby or moving subjects, parallax, and exposure differences can create seams, doubled edges, or a fallback blend. Digital zoom does not create new optical detail, and LensStack does not guarantee seamless fusion or sharper results in every scene.

Illustrative sample photos, including AI-generated examples used in App Store screenshots, demonstrate the interface. They are not evidence of measured optical improvement from a real multi-lens capture.

Permissions and troubleshooting

If Camera access is denied, enable it in iPhone Settings. LensStack requests add-only Photos permission when you save; it does not request permission to read your Photos library. If a capture is interrupted or an export fails, retry after the camera is available and check the relevant permission in Settings.

Trial and purchases

The trial is a US$0.00 non-consumable App Store purchase called 14-day Trial. It lasts 14 days from its original App Store purchase date. It does not automatically renew, convert to a paid purchase, or charge you at the end.

The lifetime unlock is a separate, one-time non-consumable purchase priced at US$9.99 in the United States. The App Store displays the applicable local price before you confirm a purchase. There is no subscription or recurring billing.

All capture, viewing, editing, and export tools require an active trial or the lifetime unlock. After the trial ends, these tools are locked; existing app-local originals are preserved until you unlock, delete the app, or replace the stack after regaining access. Support, privacy information, and Restore purchases remain accessible without an active trial or unlock.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring a trial or reinstalling the app does not restart its original 14-day period. A lifetime unlock restores through the App Store, and a refunded or revoked purchase no longer grants access.

If App Store offers are unavailable, check your connection and use Retry App Store. A previously verified lifetime unlock does not depend on loading product prices successfully. Contact support if access does not update after a completed purchase or restore.

Purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple. For purchase assistance, visit Apple billing support.

Privacy policy

LensStack by MindCoreAI processes captured photos on your device. The developer does not operate a photo-processing server, app account system, advertising service, or analytics service for LensStack.

Photos and camera access

LensStack uses the camera with your permission to capture photos from the rear cameras available on your iPhone. Alignment, blending, viewing, and reframing happen locally. The app does not upload your photos to a developer-operated server or collect advertising identifiers or in-app analytics.

Add-only Photos access is requested when you choose to export an image. The app does not request read access to your Photos library. You can revoke Camera or Photos permission in iPhone Settings; doing so does not delete existing local originals or images already saved to Photos.

Local storage and retention

The latest editable capture, its lens originals, app preferences, and a clock value used to check trial expiry are stored locally in the app's container. A successful new capture replaces the previous editable stack. Trial expiry or loss of purchase access locks the tools but does not itself delete the stored originals.

Removing the app removes this local data from the device, subject to backups you later restore. Images exported to Photos are independent and remain there until you delete them in Photos. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete your app-local photos.

Photos, sharing, and Apple backups

App data and exported Photos may be included in device backups or synced by Apple, including through iCloud, according to your settings. Images you export can be shared through other apps at your discretion. Those services and destinations operate under their own privacy policies.

App Store purchases

The free trial and lifetime unlock are handled by Apple's App Store. LensStack checks verified App Store transactions on your device to determine access and the original trial end date. The developer does not receive your payment card details or transmit your purchase history to a developer-operated server.

Apple processes Apple Account, billing, transaction, and device-service information independently under the Apple Privacy Policy.
