Static support, privacy and terms for the current free pilot. Contact email comes from the user's Future iOS App Playbook; Zhe Wu is the observed App Store account/provider identity.

Expected publication path: `https://mindcoreai.github.io/app-pages/little-wins/`.

App Store URL anchors:

- Support: `/little-wins/#support`
- Privacy: `/little-wins/#privacy`
